I — The story

Born where the Baltic gives back its amber.

Every winter, after the big storms, people walk the beaches of the Curonian Spit at first light. The sea has churned the seabed overnight, and the shore glitters with small pieces of amber — resin from forests that stood here forty million years ago.

Gintaras is our attempt to bottle that moment: cold salt air, wet pine, the faint smoke of a fire somewhere behind the dunes, and the warm, honeyed glow of amber held in a closed hand. It is not a sweet amber. It is the amber of the north — clear, resinous and a little wild.

Our perfumer spent two years and 311 trials on the formula. The brief was one sentence long: “Make it smell like dawn on the Spit in January.”

Rūta AleknaitėFounder & nose, AUŠRA
II — The notes

A pyramid in three tides.

Every fragrance unfolds in layers. The light molecules reach you first and fade fastest; the heavy ones arrive slowly and stay for hours. Gintaras was built so that each tide hands over to the next without a gap.

Top first 15 minutes

Sea saltCalabrian bergamotPink pepper

A bright, almost cold opening — like opening a window onto the sea. The pepper gives a small spark that keeps it from feeling soapy.

Heart 15 min – 3 hours

Linden blossomOrris butterHeather

Linden is the tree of every Lithuanian village. Here it is soft and honeyed, held up by powdery iris and dry heather from the dunes.

Base 3 hours and beyond

Amber accordPine resinSmoked cedarLabdanum

The warm finish that stays on scarves for days: resinous, gently smoky and glowing — the reason the fragrance carries its name.

III — On the skin

How one spray changes through the day.

Perfume is a living thing. Your skin temperature, the weather and even the fabric you wear change how it opens. This is how Gintaras typically behaves on warm skin.

0 min

Salt & spark

Citrus and sea air burst out. Wait a moment before judging — the opening is the shortest chapter.

30 min

The blossom

Linden and iris rise. The scent sits closer to the body, soft and creamy, like sunlight on a wooden wall.

3 hours

The glow

Amber and pine resin take over. People notice it when you walk past, not when you stand still.

8+ hours

The memory

A warm, smoky skin scent remains. On wool or cashmere it can still be found the next morning.

IV — The bottle

Made like a jewel.

We refused to put an amber perfume in an ordinary bottle. Every flacon is made in small batches and assembled by hand — five parts, each with its own maker.

  • Faceted capSolid zamac, hand-polished and plated in 24-carat gold. Heavy enough to feel in the palm.
  • Fine-mist atomiserReleases 0.1 ml per spray in a cloud, not a jet, so the scent settles evenly on skin.
  • Gold collarCrimped by hand. Nothing glued, so the bottle can be refilled at our boutique.
  • Thick-walled glassMouth-blown, 9 mm at the base. The weight catches light like a piece of amber.
  • The juiceAged for eight weeks in steel before bottling, so the notes marry and the colour turns to honey.

VIII — Questions

Before you choose.

Is Gintaras for women or men?

For anyone. Amber, wood and salt have no gender. Testers split almost exactly in half — some wear it on a silk scarf, others on a wool coat.

Is it very sweet?

No. The amber accord is balanced with sea salt, pine resin and smoke, so it feels warm rather than sugary. If you find most amber fragrances too heavy, try the 2 ml sample first.

Where should I apply it?

Two sprays on the pulse points — the inside of the wrists and the base of the neck — and one on clothing if you want it to last into the next day. Don't rub your wrists together: it crushes the top notes.

Does it contain real amber?

No. Fossil amber has almost no smell. “Amber” in perfumery is an accord of resins and balsams — in our case labdanum, benzoin and vanilla — built to evoke its warmth.

How should I store the bottle?

Away from sunlight and heat, ideally in its box. A bathroom is the worst place: humidity and temperature changes age perfume faster. Stored well, it keeps for five years or more.

Can I refill it?

Yes. The collar is crimped, not glued. Bring or send the bottle back and we refill it at 30% off — better for you and for the planet.
